**1. COMMENT HEALTH HAZARD**  
*(Author, Vannevar Bush, Charles Roberts, Scientist)*  
---

**Author:** Dr. Bush, your work during WWII laid the groundwork for modern American science. How do today’s NIH funding cuts compare to the priorities of your time?  
**Vannevar Bush:** Back then, we understood science as a national imperative. Federal funding gave us penicillin, the flu vaccine—even the internet. Now? Slashing budgets, freezing grants… This isn’t reform. It’s sabotage. Without foundational research, we lose *everything*—health, security, economic growth.  

**Scientist:** It’s worse than that. The NIH has stalled a billion dollars in grants since January. Labs are shutting down Alzheimer’s trials, addiction studies. PhD programs? Cancelled. My postdocs are getting pink slips.  

**Charles Roberts:** (angrily) They gutted indirect costs overnight. At St. Jude, that money covered lab waste removal, clinical trial coordinators—things that *keep kids alive*. Without it? Experimental treatments vanish. More children **will** die.  

**Author:** Meanwhile, China’s overtaking us in patents, PhDs, R&D spending…  
**Vannevar Bush:** (grim) Exactly. Tabarrok’s “invisible graveyard” grows—people dying because breakthroughs never reach them. Pancreatic cancer, dementia… How many lives will we lose waiting for a cure that’s stuck in a defunded lab?  

**Author:** And PEPFAR’s dismantled. Twenty-six million lives at risk globally.  
**Scientist:** (sarcastic) But sure, let’s trust Kennedy Jr. to push cod-liver oil over vaccines. Priorities, right?  

**Author:** What’s the endgame here?  
**Vannevar Bush:** (firm) A nation that abandons science abandons its future. This isn’t America First—it’s America *Last*.
