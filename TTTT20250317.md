### **1. COMMENT HEALTH HAZARD**  
*(Author, Vannevar Bush, Charles Roberts, Scientist, Alex Tabarrok)*  
**Author:** Dr. Bush, your WWII-era work birthed penicillin and the NSF. How do today’s NIH cuts compare?  
**Vannevar Bush:** Back then, science was a national priority. Now? Slashing grants and freezing funding—this isn’t reform. It’s strangling progress.  
**Scientist:** They’ve withheld $1B in grants since January. My Alzheimer’s trial? Dead in the water. Labs are firing postdocs. Ph.D. programs—cancelled.  
**Charles Roberts:** (angrily) St. Jude can’t afford clinical trials now. Indirect costs covered lab waste removal, patient coordinators… Kids *will* die without those.  
**Author:** And China’s overtaking us—more patents, Ph.D.s. Even R&D spending…  
**Vannevar Bush:** (grim) Invisible graveyards, Tabarrok called it. People dying because breakthroughs never happen.  
**Alex Tabarrok:** (via voiceover) Imagine a drug for pancreatic cancer stuck in a defunded lab. Those deaths are on Trump’s hands.  
**Author:** PEPFAR’s dismantled too. 26 million lives at risk.  
**Scientist:** (sarcastic) But hey, Kennedy’s pushing cod-liver oil instead of vaccines. Priorities, right?
