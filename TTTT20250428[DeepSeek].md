**Title: COMMENT COUNTING THE WAYS**  
**Participants:** *Jonathan Blitzer (Author), Nayib Bukele (President of El Salvador), Donald Trump (Former U.S. President), Stephen Miller (Deputy White House Chief of Staff), Chris Van Hollen (Maryland Senator).*
---

**Jonathan Blitzer (Author):** Let’s start with El Salvador. President Bukele, your “state of exception” led to 85,000 arrests in a country of six million. Over 360 deaths reported by Cristosal. Critics call it authoritarian. How do you respond?  

**Nayib Bukele:** Look, we liberated millions from gang terror. Sometimes they say we imprisoned thousands—*I* say we saved lives. And now? The U.S. pays us $6 million to hold their deported immigrants. *That’s* efficiency.  

**Donald Trump:** (grinning) “Liberated millions” — who wrote that line for you? Can I use it?  

**Bukele:** Be my guest. Outsourcing works. We’ll even take your criminals.  

**Trump:** (to aides) Write that down.  

**Blitzer:** But the Supreme Court ordered Kilmar Abrego Garcia’s return after an “administrative error.” He’s a Salvadoran deported after 15 years in Maryland. No criminal record.  

**Stephen Miller:** (interrupting) No mistakes here. Court ruled *for* us. He’s MS-13. End of story.  

**Chris Van Hollen:** (later, after visiting El Salvador) Abrego Garcia’s healthy? Great! Bukele says he’s “honored” to stay in custody.  

**Bukele:** (smirking) Exactly. He’s not coming back.  

**Blitzer:** So the White House ignores the Court. Meanwhile, Social Security numbers of lawful immigrants are being canceled. Assets “terminated.”  

**Trump:** (shrugs) If they’re here, they’re here illegally. Even the IRS is sharing data with ICE now.  

**Blitzer:** Undocumented immigrants pay $100 billion in taxes annually. Yale estimates $313 billion in losses if they stop filing.  

**Trump:** Who cares? Message sent.  

**Blitzer:** Legal residents are targeted too. Columbia students like Mahmoud Khalil and Yun-seo Chung—activists facing deportation.  

**Miller:** National security trumps activism.  

**Blitzer:** Judges challenge you, Bukele fires them. Trump wants to impeach Boasberg for blocking deportation flights.  

**Trump:** Judges don’t get it. We’re *winning*.  

**Blitzer:** The Fourth Circuit just ruled against you. A Reagan-appointed judge called your actions unconstitutional.  

**Trump:** (waves hand) Constitutional showdown? Bring it.  

**Blitzer:** This isn’t just about immigrants. It’s about unchecked power.  

**Bukele:** (laughing) Welcome to the new playbook.
